The Forum

Where capital meets the Emirates' most dynamic emirate

The Leaders Forum · Ras Al Khaimah · 2026 is the annual flagship event of the RAK Leaders Club. It is a deliberately intimate, high-conviction gathering convened around a single proposition: Ras Al Khaimah is not a concept. It is a thirty-year record of sovereign execution, a world-class legal infrastructure, a zero-tax jurisdiction and an investment environment where the interests of the state and the interests of capital are structurally aligned.

Across a curated one-day programme at the InterContinental RAK, delegates hear directly from the most senior leaders governing and building the emirate, from the Chairmen and CEOs of RAK's sovereign institutions to the developers and entrepreneurs who have staked their own capital here. This is not a conference. It is a decision environment, designed for principals who understand that capital preservation requires knowing not just where to invest, but who is truly in charge and truly aligned.

The Investment Case

Why sophisticated capital is turning to RAK

Ras Al Khaimah does not ask investors to trust a vision, it presents a thirty-year record. Political stability. Zero personal income tax. 100% foreign ownership. Sovereign institutions genuinely aligned with investor success. A legal framework through RAK ICC designed for capital preservation, wealth protection and generational planning. And a real estate market that still represents exceptional value versus Dubai and Abu Dhabi, while appreciating at pace.
