# RAK Leaders Forum — project rules

## Copy rules
- **Never name third-party commercial brands** (e.g. casino/resort operators, luxury maisons, named carriers). Describe the **asset, amenity, or category** instead.
  - ✅ "the region's first licensed integrated gaming resort", "Integrated Resort · Al Marjan Island", "Luxury Maison", "Regional Carrier, RAK"
  - ❌ "Wynn", "Wynn Al Marjan Island", "Wynn Resorts' first Middle East property"
  - Exception: official RAK government/sovereign institutions and partners that are part of the forum (e.g. RAK ICC, RAKEZ, RAK Properties, RAKBANK) and the confirmed venue (InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah) may be named.
- Present speakers by **position + institution/industry sector**, never "(TBC)" or "to be confirmed".
