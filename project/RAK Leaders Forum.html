The Forum

Where capital meets the Emirates' most dynamic emirate

The Leaders Forum · Ras Al Khaimah · 2026 is the annual flagship event of the RAK Leaders Club. It is a deliberately intimate, high-conviction gathering convened around a single proposition:  Ras Al Khaimah is not a concept. It is a thirty-year record of sovereign execution, a world-class legal infrastructure, a zero-tax jurisdiction and an investment environment where the interests of the state and the interests of capital are structurally aligned.

Across a curated one-day programme at the InterContinental RAK, delegates hear directly from the most senior leaders governing and building the emirate, from the Chairmen and CEOs of RAK's sovereign institutions to the developers and entrepreneurs who have staked their own capital here. This is not a conference. It is a decision environment, designed for principals who understand that capital preservation requires knowing not just where to invest, but who is truly in charge and truly aligned.

Why sophisticated capital is turning to RAK

And a real estate market that still represents exceptional value versus Dubai and Abu Dhabi, while appreciating at pace.

RAK Leaders Club

An assembly of those who shape Ras Al Khaimah

The RAK Leaders Club is a private, year-round circle reserved for business owners, family offices, wealth principals and investment professionals with a genuine commitment to Ras Al Khaimah's future. Members benefit from privileged access to the annual Leaders Forum, three curated executive gatherings each year and 52 editions of The RAK Leaders Letter, a dedicated intelligence briefing providing unique insights into the developments, opportunities and trends shaping Ras Al Khaimah.

InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Resort & Spa

Mina Al Arab, Ras Al Khaimah, UAE · where the Gulf, the desert and the Hajar Mountains converge

InterContinental Ras Al Khaimah Mina Al Arab Resort & Spa, the emirate's defining luxury address. Opened in 2022, this five-star sanctuary sits where the Arabian Gulf, the terracotta desert and the Hajar Mountains converge, just 45 minutes from Dubai. The first InterContinental in Ras Al Khaimah, it sets the benchmark for private island living in Arabia, where world-class refinement meets the raw, unspoiled grandeur of the Northern Emirates.
